Title: Plant a Tree Drive 2025

Subtitle: A win-win way to help protect and reforest Arnprior—and beyond!

What if reversing climate change was as simple as planting a tree?

Renowned Canadian botanist and filmmaker Dianna Beresford-Krueger has a very simple bioplan to do exactly that—and it’s something each of us can dig right into.

If every person on earth plants one native tree each year for the next six years, we could reforest the planet with an estimated 50 billion trees—significantly reducing carbon dioxide levels, stabilizing temperatures, and restoring balance to the carbon cycle by 2030.

It’s as easy as 1-2-3

Save the Grove Again hopes to help the community fulfill this call to arms—or, shovels—with Plant a Tree Drive 2025.

We’re holding an Eastern White Pine seedling fundraiser, with saplings available for $5 each.

🌲 Step 1: Pre-order yours now by filling out the form

🌲 Step 2: Pick up your seedlings at the Great Arnprior Trash Hunt

When: April 26, 2025 @ 9 am to noon
Where: Arnprior Museum parking lot
Bonus: You can also pick up a trash hunting kit and help clean up Arnprior!

Can’t make it on April 26? No problem—we’re happy to arrange an alternate pickup time that works for you.

🌲 Step 3: Plant those babies!

At pick up, you’ll receive simple planting and care instructions so it’s easy to get growing. Here’s a handy guide from the Arbor Day Foundation.

Each seedling is a bare root Eastern White Pine, a species native to this area and deeply rooted in Arnprior’s identity. It’s the dominant tree in Gillies Grove, our town’s cherished old-growth forest. Beresford-Kroeger calls pines “living pharmacopoeias,” thanks to natural compounds like pinosylvin, a powerful antibiotic.

Why each tree matters

This isn’t just about reforesting—it’s about reclaiming agency in the face of climate change. Tree by tree, yard by yard, balcony by balcony, greenspace by greenspace, we can all work together to take back the landscape from deforestation. When you plant a tree, you’re helping cool the air, purify water, anchor soil, and create habitat for birds and pollinators.

You’re also helping protect Gillies Grove, a sanctuary of biodiversity that continues to be threatened by nearby development.

“If you plant a tree, you are investing in the future of the planet. You are giving hope.”
- Beresford-Kroeger

Together, we can make a difference.
Together, we can choose another way.
